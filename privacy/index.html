Privacy.

These are the working privacy principles for the Vixera AI website and products. This is a preliminary version — it will be finalized before public launch, and any change will be visible on this page.

Last updated: September 2026

What this covers

This page covers this website — vixera.ai and its pages, including the waitlist and contact forms. It states how we handle the little information the site touches, and how product privacy will work when each product launches.

Data we collect on this site

  • Automatically: nothing beyond standard server logs. Like nearly every website, the server that delivers these pages records basic technical logs (such as the page requested and the time of the request) to keep the site running and secure. We do not use them to profile you.
  • What you choose to send us. If you join a waitlist or contact us, the form collects what you type — typically your email address and your message. Nothing is collected unless you submit. In the current preview, submissions are stored locally in your own browser and are not yet transmitted to Vixera AI; that changes when form delivery is connected before launch, and this policy will be updated when it does.

How we use it

We use what you send us to do the thing you asked for — reply to your message, or tell you about the product you joined a waitlist for. Nothing else. We do not sell your data, and we do not use waitlist or contact submissions for unrelated marketing.

Product data

Each Vixera AI product will carry its own privacy policy at launch, specific to the data that product actually handles. Across the family, products are designed around the same principles: explicit permission before a connection is made, and revocation that actually revokes. Read more about that approach on our security page.

Your choices

Once form delivery is connected, if you have submitted your email through this site and want it removed, ask us via the contact page and we will delete it. In the current preview nothing reaches us, so there is nothing for us to hold — submissions live only in your own browser, and you can clear them by clearing this site's data.

Updates

This page will change as products launch and their policies are finalized. Every revision will carry a visible date at the top, so you can always tell which version you are reading.
