Trust

Connected by permission.

Vixera One deals with the most personal data there is. The architecture is being designed around one principle: you decide what it sees, and you can take it back.

You control every connection
Services are connected individually, on purpose. Nothing is linked by default, and access can be revoked at any time.
OAuth, not passwords
Where a service supports it, Vixera One is designed to use OAuth with the minimum necessary scopes — your credentials are never exposed to the interface.
Finance through secure providers
Financial connections are planned through regulated providers such as Plaid. Vixera One will never ask for your online banking password.
Approval before action
Anything that spends money or sends on your behalf — orders, payments, messages — is designed to require your explicit confirmation first.
