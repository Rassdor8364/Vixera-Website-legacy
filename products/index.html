Business Operating System

Vixera

Live product

Vixera is a multi-tenant operating system for operationally complex businesses — live today and used daily where inventory, people and deadlines collide, such as trade-show warehouses. It holds the state of the operation in one system: what exists, where it is, who is working on it, and what happens next — infrastructure for running a business, not another dashboard on top of one.

Inventory that reflects reality
Stock, locations and movement tracked across warehouses and sites, so the system's picture and the physical world stay in agreement.
Operations, coordinated
Jobs, workflows and teams organized in one place — the work itself, not just records about the work.
Multi-tenant by design
One platform serving many organizations, each with its own data, users and configuration.
Broad by intent
Built as general business infrastructure: the same operational core adapts to different industries instead of locking into one vertical.

Document Intelligence

Praxion

Edit. Understand. Sign. Convert. Secure.

Praxion by Vixera AI treats documents as living information, not static files. It reads a document the way a specialist would — structure, meaning, what matters — and gives you the tools to act on it directly. One standard governs everything it does: precision in every document action.

Edit
Change content and structure in place, with the document's integrity preserved through every revision.
Understand
Extract structure and meaning from what a file actually says — so a contract, an invoice and a report are treated as what they are.
Sign & secure
Signatures, protection and access control handled inside the same workflow as the document itself.
Convert
Move between formats without losing what makes the document itself — layout, structure and meaning survive the trip.

Home Intelligence

Pantrix

Intelligence for the home.

Pantrix brings operational intelligence to the environment with the least tooling: home. It understands what a household has, what it uses, and what runs out — so the running inventory of domestic life stops living in your head.

Know what you have
A live picture of the household — pantry, supplies and essentials — instead of a mental checklist rebuilt every week.
See what runs out
Usage patterns surface what is running low before it is gone, turning grocery panic into a quiet heads-up.
Replenish on your terms
Automated purchasing is planned — and approval-first by design. Pantrix will never place an order without your explicit confirmation.

Predictive & Financial Intelligence

Praevion

See before it happens.

Praevion is built on quantitative honesty: probabilities, not promises. It watches markets, news and events for signals, builds scenarios around them, and states its uncertainty plainly — so you can reason about risk before it becomes an outcome.

Signals, not noise
Movements across markets, news and events, filtered down to the ones that change the picture.
Scenarios with probabilities
Possible outcomes expressed as likelihoods, so a forecast is something you can weigh — not something you have to believe.
Uncertainty, stated
Every scenario carries its confidence. Nothing is presented as a guaranteed outcome, because nothing is.
