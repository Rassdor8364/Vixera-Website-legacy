Business Operating System

The operating system
for modern business.

Vixera brings operations, inventory, people, workflows and intelligence into one system — built for operationally complex businesses like warehouses, gyms, restaurants, logistics and events, and architected as broader business infrastructure.

The platform

One system,
whole operation.

Most businesses run on a patchwork of spreadsheets, chat threads and disconnected tools. Vixera replaces the patchwork with one system of record that knows what you have, who is doing what, and what happens next.

Operations
Jobs, shipments and warehouse work are planned, assigned and tracked in one place — from intake to outbound.
People
Teams, roles and permissions live in the same system as the work, so everyone sees exactly what is theirs to do.
Inventory
Items, containers, shipments and locations tracked as one living system — not rows in a spreadsheet that go stale.
Analytics
Stock levels, throughput and utilization are read directly from live operations, not reconstructed from exports.
Automation
Status changes, routine updates and notifications happen as work happens, so the record keeps itself current.
Intelligence
An intelligence layer over your own operational data — ask about your operation and get answers grounded in its actual state.

Why it works

Built from real operations.

Vixera is live software running real multi-location inventory and warehouse operations today — for operationally complex businesses such as trade-show warehouses, where thousands of items move between storage, docks and events on hard deadlines.

That operational core is the foundation. The platform is expanding from it into broader business infrastructure — the same system of record, applied to more of how a business runs.
