What Praxion does

Every document action.
One system.

Six verbs cover almost everything anyone does with a document. Praxion is being designed around exactly those six — nothing more.

Edit
Designed to change text, reorder pages, merge, split and redact — directly in the document, without round-tripping through other tools.
Convert
Built to move between PDF, Word, images and archival formats while keeping layout, structure and fonts intact.
Understand
OCR, structure extraction and AI analysis designed to turn documents into data — fields, tables and clauses you can search and query.
Organize
Versions, folders and metadata designed to keep large collections navigable — every document findable, and its history visible.
Sign
Designed to place signature fields, request signatures and track completion, with a clear record of who signed what and when.
Automate
Rules for repetitive document work — renaming, routing, conversion — are being built so routine handling runs without you.

Principles

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Praxion is built on three commitments. Speed — documents open and respond immediately, whatever their size. Clarity — one interface for every document action, with nothing buried three menus deep. Security — encryption in transit and at rest as design principles, because documents carry contracts, invoices and identities, not just words.
