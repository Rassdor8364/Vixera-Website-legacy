What Pantrix does

Track. Predict. Plan. Replenish.

Four verbs, one loop. A household runs on inventory and recurring demand — Pantrix is built to close that loop calmly.

Track
Pantry, fridge and household inventory in one place — quantities, locations and expiry dates, kept current with as little effort from you as possible.
Predict
Usage curves and expiry windows. Pantrix is designed to learn how quickly your household actually moves through things, so "running low" becomes a forecast instead of a surprise.
Plan
Meals and recipes built from what you actually have — starting with what expires first — rather than an aspirational list that ignores your shelves.
Replenish
Pantrix drafts the shopping list before you think to write it. And if automated ordering ever ships, an order is only ever placed after you approve it.
