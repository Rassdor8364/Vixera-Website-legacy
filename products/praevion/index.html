Capabilities

Evidence in.
Calibrated probability out.

Every capability follows the same discipline: gather evidence, weigh it, state a probability, and state how much that probability should be trusted.

Market intelligence
Designed to read prices, spreads and implied probabilities as a record of collective expectation — and to show where the evidence supports that expectation, and where it does not.
Probability
Estimates are expressed as probabilities with confidence ranges, never as verdicts. Calibration is the standard: over time, events rated 60% should resolve true about 60% of the time.
Signals
Structured signals drawn from prices, data releases and event streams — each weighted by its historical predictive value, and discounted as the evidence behind it ages.
News & events
Designed to treat news as evidence, not noise: extract the claim, date it, weigh the source, and update only the probabilities that claim actually bears on.
Scenarios
Branching scenario analysis with explicit assumptions. Change one assumption and the downstream probabilities move with it — uncertainty stays visible at every step.
Risk
Risk expressed as distributions and ranges of outcomes, not a single number — designed to surface concentration, correlation and tail exposure before they compound.

The long view

The predictive arm of Vixera AI.

Praevion is being built as the predictive arm of the Vixera AI ecosystem, and is planned to grow into broader financial intelligence: portfolio intelligence, risk analysis, macro data, scenario analysis and research assistance. The direction is constant — quantitative rigor, stated uncertainty, and analysis a careful reader can interrogate.

Praevion provides analysis and probabilistic information, not financial advice. Markets involve risk, including the loss of capital, and probabilities describe uncertainty — they do not remove it. Nothing on this page is a recommendation to buy or sell any asset, or a guarantee of any outcome.
